## Descripción de la tarea

Congratulations, you are admitted to the Summer School on "Higher-orderFourier analysis and related topics" at Kopp, Germany, October  4 - 9,2026.
You will find your assigned topic in the list of topics at the webpage https://math-events.uni-bonn.de/event/343/ We have added talks as we accepted more participants than planned,thereby partially changing the numbering. Please note that most of the assignments come with instructions in the topics file. Please feel free to contact either of the organizers with scientific questions on the topics.
Here is how the summer school works: prior to the summer school, you have to:
1) Prepare one presentation (45min) on the topic assigned to you.
2) Write a 3 - 5 page summary using our latex templatehttps://math-events.uni-bonn.de/event/343/materials on your topic.
Please send the *summaries* electronically to thiele@math.uni-bonn.dewith cc to dhachen@math.uni-bonn.de *by August 28, 2026* at the latest to allow time for paper processing.
The summaries will be put together into a proceedings volume of the summer school and traditionally hardcopies will be distributed at the summer school. You can find samples of these proceedings on the webpageof previous summer schools http://www.math.uni-bonn.de/people/thiele/
